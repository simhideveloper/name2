Install as an app

  • iPhone or iPad: in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Mac: in Safari, File → Add to Dock.
  • Chrome or Edge: use the install icon at the right end of the address bar.

How to play

  • Two players share this screen. X goes first.
  • Each turn: place your mark on any empty square on any face, then twist one layer a quarter turn. Any face or any middle slice counts.
  • Marks ride along with their squares. Three in a row, column or diagonal on a single face wins; lines are checked after each placement and each twist.
  • A twist that completes only your opponent's line loses. Lines for both means the twister wins. A full cube with no line is a draw.

Controls

  • Tap a square on the cube or the map to place.
  • Swipe across the cube with one finger to twist the layer under it. The middle row or column twists a middle slice.
  • Drag the background or use two fingers to move only the view.
  • Lock view snaps face-on to the nearest face. While locked, background drags and two-finger swipes turn the cube to the next face; the bar under the cube twists the face you see.
  • Faces are named by position (Top, Front, Right…); the labels stay put while layers turn.
