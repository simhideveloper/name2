Rules
  • Two players share this screen and alternate turns. X goes first.
  • On your turn, first place your mark on any empty square on any face (tap the cube or the unfolded map).
  • Then you must twist one layer a quarter turn, in either direction: any of the six faces, or one of the three middle slices. Marks ride along with their squares.
  • Faces are named by position (Top, Front, Right…); the floating labels stay put while the cube turns. A middle slice turns in the same sense as the face it is named after.
  • Swipe across the cube with one finger (or the mouse) to twist the layer under your finger in that direction; the middle row or column twists the middle slice. Drag the background, or use two fingers, to move only the view.
  • Lock view snaps the camera face-on to the nearest face and centres it. While locked, dragging the background or swiping with two fingers turns the whole cube to the next face (arrow keys work too). The buttons under the cube twist the face you are looking at.
  • Three of your marks in a row, column or diagonal on any single face wins. Lines are checked after each placement and after each twist.
  • If your twist completes a line for your opponent but not for you, your opponent wins. If it completes lines for both, you win.
  • All 54 squares full with no line is a draw. Undo (or Ctrl/Cmd+Z) takes back the last action.
